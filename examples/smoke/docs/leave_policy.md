# Shore Staff Leave Policy (v3.2, effective 1 April 2026)

## Annual leave
Shore staff accrue annual leave monthly. Entitlement depends on grade and is listed in
leave_entitlements.csv. Unused annual leave of up to 10 days may be carried into the next
calendar year; anything above 10 days lapses on 31 December.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate is
required for any absence longer than 2 consecutive working days. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Applying for leave
Leave requests are submitted in the HR portal at least 14 days in advance for annual leave.
Emergency leave can be applied for retrospectively within 3 working days of returning.
The reporting manager approves or rejects requests within 5 working days.
