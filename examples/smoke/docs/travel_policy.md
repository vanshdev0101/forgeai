# Business Travel Policy (v1.4)

## Daily allowance
Shore staff travelling for work receive a daily allowance of INR 2,500 for domestic travel and
USD 90 for international travel. The allowance covers meals and local transport.

## Flights
Economy class is mandatory for flights under 6 hours. Flights of 6 hours or longer may be booked
in premium economy with Head of Department approval.

## Claims
Expense claims must be filed within 30 days of returning, with original receipts attached.
Claims filed after 30 days require Finance Director approval.
