# Shore Staff Leave Policy
**Effective Date:** Immediately upon issuance | **Applicability:** All shore staff grades at Meridian Ship Management (Mumbai and Singapore offices)

This policy outlines the entitlements, accrual methods, application procedures, and approval timelines for all forms of paid leave available to shore-based employees. It applies uniformly across our Mumbai and Singapore shore operations regardless of grade or tenure.

## Annual Leave Entitlement and Accrual
Employees are granted annual leave based on their specific job grade at the time of employment renewal. The yearly entitlement is fixed as follows:
- G1 Associate: 18 days
- G2 Senior Associate: 21 days
- G3 Manager: 24 days
- G4 Senior Manager: 27 days
- G5 Director: 30 days

Leave accrues monthly at a rate of one twelfth of the yearly entitlement. Unused annual leave may be carried into the next calendar year, provided the total does not exceed ten days. Any carried-over leave above ten days lapses on thirty-one December and will no longer be available for use in subsequent periods.

## Sick Leave Policy
All employees are entitled to twelve days of paid sick leave per calendar year. To utilize this entitlement beyond two consecutive working days, a medical certificate is required from an authorized healthcare provider. Failure to provide such documentation may result in the classification of absence as unpaid or unauthorized depending on company disciplinary procedures.

## Parental Leave Entitlements
Female employees are eligible for twenty-six weeks of paid maternity leave following childbirth. Male employees receive ten working days of paid paternity leave, which must be taken within three months of the birth to remain valid under this policy.

## Bereavement Leave
In the event of a death within an immediate family member's circle, five days of paid bereavement leave are granted automatically upon notification and verification by Human Resources. This entitlement covers spouses, parents, children, siblings, and registered domestic partners.

## Application Procedures and Timelines
To request annual leave or other planned absences, employees must submit a formal request through the internal portal at least fourteen days in advance of the intended start date. Requests submitted outside this window will be evaluated on an exceptional basis subject to operational constraints.

For unforeseen circumstances requiring emergency leave, employees may apply retrospectively within three working days of returning to duty. Such requests require supporting documentation where applicable and must include a detailed explanation of the situation via email or HR ticketing system.

## Approval Authority and Review Periods
All leave requests are routed through the employee's direct reporting manager for initial assessment. The reporting manager approves or rejects leave requests within five working days from receipt of the formal application. Managers may consult with department heads if operational impact is significant, but final approval authority rests solely with the immediate supervisor.

## Administrative Notes
This document supersedes previous internal memos regarding shore staff leave policies effective immediately. HR departments in Mumbai and Singapore will update employee records accordingly upon implementation. Any discrepancies between this policy and individual employment contracts shall be resolved by reference to this current directive unless otherwise amended through a formal board resolution.

## Key figures

- Carried-over leave above 10 days lapses on 31 December.
- 26 weeks of paid maternity leave.
