# Business Travel and Expense Policy
**Effective Date:** Immediate | **Applicability:** All Shore Staff Grades (Mumbai & Singapore Offices)

This policy establishes the guidelines for business travel arrangements, expense claims, and reimbursement standards applicable to all employees of Meridian Ship Management. These rules apply equally to staff based in our Mumbai shore office and those operating from our Singapore headquarters or on international assignments involving both locations.\nThe primary objective is to ensure that all work-related expenditures are managed efficiently while maintaining appropriate cost controls without compromising employee comfort or safety during travel duties.

### 1. Flight Classifications and Travel Time Limits
Employees must adhere strictly to the following flight class requirements based on journey duration:
- **Economy Class:** This category applies exclusively to flights with a total flying time of under six hours. All staff, regardless of grade, are required to book economy tickets for these shorter journeys.
- **Premium Economy:** For any flight segment lasting six hours or longer, employees must select premium economy class. While this is the standard requirement for long-haul travel involving our international operations between Mumbai and Singapore and beyond, exceptions may be considered only with explicit approval from a Head of Department prior to booking.

### 2. Daily Allowance Standards
A daily allowance is provided to cover incidental costs such as meals and local transport during business trips. The specific amounts are determined by the nature of the travel:
- **Domestic Travel:** For all journeys occurring entirely within India, staff receive a daily allowance of INR 2,500.
- **International Travel:** When traveling outside Indian borders or on international assignments involving our global network, the standard daily allowance is USD 90 per day.

### 3. Accommodation Limits
Hotel bookings must align with the following nightly limits based on the location of the stay:
- **Metro Cities:** In major metropolitan areas defined by local government standards, the maximum permissible hotel rate is INR 7,000 per night.
- **Elsewhere in India:** For stays outside designated metro cities across the country, including remote ports and regional hubs, the limit reduces to INR 4,500 per night.

### 4. Personal Vehicle Usage Reimbursement
In specific circumstances where public transport is unavailable or impractical for official duties within domestic jurisdictions, employees may use their personal vehicles. The reimbursement rate for fuel and wear-and-tear in these instances is fixed at INR 12 per kilometer traveled. This applies strictly to approved business routes; any deviation from the authorized itinerary will not be covered under this policy.

### 5. Expense Claim Procedures and Documentation
To ensure financial compliance, all expense claims must follow strict documentation and timing protocols:
- **Receipt Requirements:** Original physical or digital receipts are mandatory for reimbursement of any single expense exceeding INR 500. Expenses below this threshold may be recorded via internal logs but require supervisor verification.
- **Submission Timeline:** All expense claims must be filed within thirty days of the employee's return from a work trip. Failure to submit claims by this deadline will result in automatic denial unless special permission is granted.

### 6. Late Submission Protocol
If an employee realizes they have missed the standard submission window, or if additional documentation becomes available after the initial filing period, claims filed after thirty days require direct approval from the Finance Director before any reimbursement can be processed. This step ensures that all financial records remain accurate and auditable.

### 7. General Conduct
Staff are expected to act with integrity regarding these policies. Any attempt to inflate mileage logs, submit duplicate receipts for expenses above INR 500 without original proof, or book accommodation exceeding the stated limits will be treated as a serious violation of company conduct standards and may lead to disciplinary action.

By adhering to this policy, Meridian Ship Management ensures that our shore staff in Mumbai, Singapore, and all operational locations are supported fairly while maintaining fiscal responsibility across our global fleet management operations.

## Key figures

- Use of a personal car is reimbursed at INR 12 per km.
