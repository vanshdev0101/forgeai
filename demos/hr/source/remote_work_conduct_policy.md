# Remote Work and Code of Conduct

**Effective Date:** Immediate | **Version:** 1.0

## Purpose
This policy establishes the framework for shore staff at Meridian Ship Management to perform their duties effectively, whether working from our offices in Mumbai or Singapore or from approved remote locations. It outlines eligibility criteria, equipment support, and mandatory conduct standards applicable to all grades of employees.

## Eligibility and Approval Process
Remote work is permitted strictly **up to 2 days per week**, contingent upon explicit approval from the reporting manager. Staff must submit a formal request detailing their proposed schedule and justification for remote operation. The reporting manager retains full discretion to approve or deny requests based on operational requirements, project deadlines, and team dynamics. Approval is not retroactive; work performed without prior authorization does not count toward the weekly allowance.

## Core Working Hours
While flexibility exists regarding start times, all staff must adhere to **core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 IST** during days designated for remote work or when collaborating with cross-timezone teams. During these core hours, employees must be fully available via digital communication channels and ready to participate in scheduled meetings without interruption. Outside of these windows, staff may manage their schedules as long as deliverables are met and availability is maintained.

## Home Office Equipment Allowance
To support a functional home office environment, Meridian Ship Management provides **a home-office equipment allowance of INR 15,000 once every 3 years**. This lump sum can be utilized for purchasing ergonomic furniture, reliable internet connectivity upgrades, or necessary hardware such as laptops and monitors. Requests to utilize this fund must be submitted through the finance portal with receipts attached upon completion of purchases. The three-year eligibility window resets automatically based on the employee's tenure; however, any unspent balance from a previous cycle does not carry forward.

## Gifts and Hospitality Policy
Staff interacting with external vendors, clients, or regulatory bodies must adhere to strict gift-giving guidelines. **Gifts above INR 2,000 must be declared to HR** immediately upon receipt. This threshold applies regardless of the form of the gift, whether cash, vouchers, or physical items. Declared gifts exceeding this limit will typically require management approval before acceptance and may need to be returned if they create a perception of impropriety. All interactions involving potential conflicts in value must be documented transparently.

## Conflicts of Interest
Employees have an ongoing duty to identify situations where personal interests might interfere with professional obligations. **Conflicts of interest must be declared within 7 days of becoming aware of them**. This includes familial relationships, financial investments in vendor companies, or any other external commitments that could influence decision-making. Failure to disclose a conflict promptly may result in disciplinary action and does not absolve the employee of liability for subsequent breaches.

## Harassment and Discrimination
Meridian Ship Management maintains a zero-tolerance stance toward all forms of harassment and discrimination. Any allegations or observations regarding inappropriate behavior must be reported confidentially through established channels. **Harassment complaints go to the Internal Committee within 3 months of the incident**. While we encourage immediate reporting, this timeline ensures that investigations can still proceed effectively while respecting legal constraints on evidence retention. The Internal Committee will ensure a fair and impartial investigation process for all complainants.

## Confidential Reporting Channels
To foster an open culture where concerns are addressed safely, **the confidential ethics hotline is open 24 hours a day**. This channel accepts anonymous tips regarding safety violations, financial irregularities, or ethical breaches. Reports submitted via the hotline trigger immediate review by senior leadership and relevant compliance officers without fear of retaliation.

## Conclusion
Adherence to this policy ensures operational excellence and upholds Meridian Ship Management's reputation as an industry leader. Managers are responsible for enforcing these guidelines consistently across Mumbai and Singapore offices, while HR provides ongoing support regarding interpretation and implementation.
