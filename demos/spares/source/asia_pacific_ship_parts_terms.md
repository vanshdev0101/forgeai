# Asia Pacific Ship Parts: Terms of Supply

**Effective Date:** Immediate | **Applicable Region:** Singapore Operations

## Purpose and Scope
This document outlines the commercial terms governing the supply of ship spare parts by Meridian Ship Management's authorized supplier, Asia Pacific Ship Parts (Singapore). These provisions apply to all vessel superintendents and purchasing staff engaged with our shore offices in Mumbai and Singapore. Adherence to these terms is mandatory for all procurement activities within the defined region.

## Payment Conditions
All commercial transactions must strictly adhere to the following payment schedule: a 50% advance payment is required prior to order processing, with the remaining balance due upon delivery of goods. Failure to meet this financial obligation may result in shipment delays or contract termination. Invoices will be issued accordingly based on these split-payment requirements.

## Pricing and Discounts
Asia Pacific Ship Parts maintains competitive pricing structures designed to optimize operational budgets for our fleet. A 3% discount applies automatically to orders exceeding USD 5,000 in total value. This threshold is calculated at the time of order confirmation. Orders falling below this monetary limit will be processed at standard listed rates without reduction.

## Delivery Terms and Logistics
All shipments are executed under delivery terms defined as CIF Singapore (Cost, Insurance, and Freight). Responsibility for goods transfers to the buyer upon arrival in Singapore, inclusive of insurance coverage during transit. For items currently held in stock, we commit to shipping within 2 working days from receipt of cleared payment.

## Urgent Service Requirements
In instances where immediate availability is critical, urgent air freight services may be arranged at an additional cost. An urgent air freight surcharge adds a 20% charge on the order value for expedited shipments via air cargo. This fee covers accelerated handling and priority logistics coordination required to meet tight turnaround times.

## Warranty Coverage
All supplied parts carry a comprehensive warranty period of six months from the date of delivery in Singapore. During this duration, any defects arising from manufacturing or material issues will be addressed under our standard warranty protocol. Claims must be submitted with supporting documentation within the valid window.

## Return Policy and Restocking Fees
Returns are accepted exclusively if requested within 14 days of receipt of goods by the consignee in Singapore. Items returned outside this timeframe will not qualify for a refund or exchange. Furthermore, all returns incur a restocking fee equal to 15% of the original invoice value to cover administrative processing and reconditioning costs.

## Operational Guidelines for Superintendents
Vessel superintendents are expected to coordinate closely with shore offices in Mumbai before initiating any procurement requests involving Asia Pacific Ship Parts. Ensure that all technical specifications match approved lists prior to placing orders exceeding the discount threshold or requiring urgent air freight services. Maintain accurate records of delivery receipts and warranty certificates for future reference.

## Contact Information
For inquiries regarding order status, warranty claims, or return authorizations, please direct correspondence through the designated purchasing staff channels in Singapore. All communications should explicitly reference this document to ensure proper alignment with current commercial terms.
