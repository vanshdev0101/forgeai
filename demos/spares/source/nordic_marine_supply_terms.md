# Nordic Marine Supply
**Effective Date:** Immediate | **Applicable To:** Meridian Ship Management Vessels and Shore Offices (Mumbai, Singapore)

## Purpose and Scope
This document establishes the commercial terms governing all supply agreements between Nordic Marine Supply (Rotterdam) and Meridian Ship Management. These provisions apply exclusively to orders for ship spare parts placed by vessel superintendents or purchasing staff located at our shore offices in Mumbai and Singapore. All transactions must adhere strictly to the conditions outlined herein.

## Payment Obligations
Invoices issued by Nordic Marine Supply are payable within **Net 30 days from invoice**. Failure to settle accounts within this window may result in suspension of supply services until outstanding balances are cleared. Payments should be directed through standard banking channels as specified on each commercial document, ensuring that all remittances reference the specific purchase order number.

## Volume Discount Structure
To encourage consolidated procurement and optimize inventory management across our fleet, a tiered volume discount structure is applied to total order values:
- A **5% volume discount** applies to orders exceeding **USD 10,000**.
- An **8% volume discount** applies to orders exceeding **USD 25,000**.

These discounts are calculated on the net subtotal of eligible items before freight and surcharges. Staff members must ensure that order aggregations accurately reflect these thresholds prior to submission for approval.

| Order Value Range | Discount Applied |
| :--- | :--- |
| Up to USD 10,000 | No discount |
| Above USD 10,000 | 5% |
| Above USD 25,000 | 8% |

## Delivery and Logistics
Standard delivery terms for all spare parts orders are **DAP (Delivered At Place) to the vessel's port**. This means Nordic Marine Supply bears all risks and costs associated with transporting goods until they arrive at the designated destination port. 

For items currently held in stock, we commit to shipping within **3 working days** of order confirmation. Please note that this timeline assumes standard freight arrangements; expedited methods incur additional charges as detailed below.

## Urgent Service and Surcharges
Meridian Ship Management may require immediate parts delivery due to operational emergencies or critical vessel needs. In such instances, urgent air freight can be arranged upon request. However, utilizing this service triggers an **urgent air freight adds a 15% surcharge on the order value**. This fee is non-negotiable and applies regardless of whether volume discounts are active for that specific shipment.

## Warranty Coverage
All spare parts supplied under these terms carry a **parts carry a 12-month warranty** against defects in material or workmanship. The warranty period commences from the date of delivery to the vessel's port. Claims must be submitted with supporting documentation, including photos and serial numbers, within the validity window.

## Returns Policy
While we strive for perfect order accuracy, limited returns are permitted under strict conditions. **Returns are accepted within 30 days** of receipt at the destination port. Any goods returned to Nordic Marine Supply will incur a restocking fee equal to **10%** of the original invoice value. This fee covers administrative processing and potential depreciation of inventory upon return.

## Compliance Requirements
Vessel superintendents and purchasing staff are responsible for verifying part compatibility before finalizing orders. Non-compliance with these terms, including unauthorized deviations from payment schedules or misrepresentation of order urgency, may result in contractual review. All queries regarding specific line items should be directed to the Nordic Marine Supply account manager prior to dispatch.

By placing an order under this agreement, Meridian Ship Management acknowledges and accepts all conditions set forth herein without further notice required.
